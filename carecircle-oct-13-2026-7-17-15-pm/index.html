Terms & Conditions

CareCircle is a family coordination tool. By continuing, you agree to use it for lawful, respectful care coordination.

Health information

CareCircle is not a medical device and does not diagnose, monitor, or recommend treatment. Health categories are entered by family members from a report or care-team guidance. For medical concerns, contact a qualified clinician; for emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Local storage and account

This MVP stores the profile, password verifier, and care records in this browser on this device. There is no server, encrypted cloud backup, or account recovery. Anyone with access to this browser profile or its local data may be able to access the records. Do not use this local lock as protection for highly sensitive information.

Family sharing and responsibility

Only enter information you are authorized to share with your family. Keep entries accurate, review source reports, and do not rely on CareCircle as the sole record for medication, appointments, or clinical decisions.

Add a family member

Remove family member?

Their local medical records, check-ins, and chat messages will be deleted. Open tasks and appointments will be reassigned to you. The care-recipient profile is kept.
